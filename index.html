How it works

Simple for creators. Powerful for brands.

For creators
1
Build your profile
Add your bio, niches, social accounts, and collaboration rates. Your profile goes live instantly.
2
Get discovered by brands
Brands search by genre, reach, and location. You appear in their discover feed automatically.
3
Receive & manage quotations
Accept, negotiate, or decline brand offers — all in one place. No more email chains.
4
Collaborate and grow
Chat, finalise deals, and build long-term brand relationships without leaving the app.
For brands & agencies
1
Search with precision
Filter by niche, platform, follower count, location, and engagement rate in seconds.
2
Unlock profiles with credits
Preview creators free. Spend 1 credit to unlock contact info and send a quotation.
3
Send quotations directly
No agencies. Send structured collaboration offers with budget, timeline, and deliverables.
4
Manage everything in one place
Track outreach, conversations, and accepted deals from one simple dashboard.
